## What an advance copy is

*Orbital Ring Engineering, Volume I: Space Mass Transit Complete Design Review* goes on sale on 19 January 2027. Before then, a limited number of advance reader copies go out to people who will read it with a pencil in hand. The copy is the complete book: nineteen chapters, the appendix on the simulation code, the glossary and the index, 450 pages, as a PDF or an EPUB sent to your device through BookFunnel. It is free, and it stays yours.

## It comes with a Reader's Guide

Every advance copy comes with *A Reader's Guide to Orbital Ring Engineering, Volume I*. It is a short companion for a reader with limited time. It says what each chapter is about, where its main numbers are, and what the book itself says is still open, and it names the seven chapters to read in full. The guide points into the book and does not replace it.

## What Paul asks in return

Nothing is required. Two things are welcome.

**Find the errors.** This is an engineering book with a lot of numbers in it, and the first readers are the best check it will ever get. If something does not add up, report it on the [errata page](/errata/); the correction goes in front of every other reader, with your name on it if you want it there.

**If you feel like it, say what you think.** An honest review on [Goodreads](/review/) can go up any time; Amazon takes reviews from 19 January. The review is optional, no rating is expected, nothing is given in return for one, and if you write one, say that you received a free advance copy. Those are Amazon's rules and the law, and they are also just fair.

## Who the copies are for

- Engineers and scientists who will read the derivations, not around them
- Students of physics, aerospace or engineering, and the people who teach them
- Reviewers, bloggers and podcasters who cover space, engineering or hard science fiction
- Readers of Isaac Arthur, Andy Weir and Kim Stanley Robinson who want the numbers behind the story

If you fit more than one, say so below.

## How it works

1. Apply with the form. It takes a minute; the last question is optional, and a sentence or two there helps.
2. A confirmation email arrives first. Click it, or nothing else happens.
3. Paul reads every application and chooses the group. Copies go out from early December, with a link to download the book in the format you chose and the Reader's Guide.
4. Two short emails follow during the reading period, and one when the book is on sale. You can leave the list at any time with the link at the bottom of any of them.
