# Thank you for reading

If you have a few minutes, I would be grateful if you left an honest review of the book.

Reviews are how other engineers and students find these books. Most of the people who could use them will never hear about them any other way. Reviews also make it possible for me to keep writing the series.

The review does not need to be long. A few sentences on what worked for you, what did not, and who you think should read it is plenty. Critical reviews are welcome too. If you found a mistake, please also report it on the [errata page](/errata/) so I can fix it.

Pick the store where you bought the book, or the one you use most. Each button takes you straight to the review form.
