## About the Book

*Orbital Ring Engineering* is a deep dive into the physics, material science, and engineering required to construct an orbital ring. The book presents a set of assumptions and guides the reader through a discovery process, delving deeply into the various materials, physics, electrical engineering, orbital mechanics, and mathematics involved in this endeavor.

It invites readers to think about the challenges and contribute to the innovations. What are the optimal numbers? Are the assumptions correct? The book lays out the design concepts and walks through the findings step by step.

## What's Inside

The first edition runs to eight chapters across three sections:

- **Section 1 — Introduction and historical overview.** The evolution into space and the case for an orbital ring.
- **Section 2 — Technical design.** Escaping the gravity well, momentum transfer and levitation, returning to Earth, anchor lines, and accelerating and deploying the ring.
- **Section 3 — Applications and context.** Utilization, economics, and the political reasoning behind building one.

## A Note on This Edition

This is the original single-volume edition. The work has since been expanded into a three-volume edition, *Engineering Orbital Rings, Mass Drivers and Space Elevators*, with significantly more detail and updated analysis. If you are buying for the first time, you may prefer the new edition.
