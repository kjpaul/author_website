## Overview

Volume II lays the groundwork the rest of the series builds on: the orbital mechanics of rings, tethers and launch trajectories, the materials science of the carbon nanotube cable and the superconducting tape, and the electrical systems that move power and current around a structure the size of a planet. Every result is derived, and every derivation is shown.
