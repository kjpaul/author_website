## Overview

Volume I is a complete design review of an orbital ring system with integrated mass driver launch and space elevator. The volume covers materials selection, structural analysis, power generation and transmission, control systems, orbital mechanics, construction sequencing, and the failure modes you have to design against.

Volumes II and III establish the first principles and the comparative analysis of the launch technologies; Volume I commits to a specific design and works through it. Every number comes from a run of the open-source simulation code, and the references for every numbered citation are published on this site.

## What's Inside

Nineteen chapters, each a complete subsystem: the ring and its orbit, the cable, the design altitude, the radiation environment, superconductors and currents, the levitation system, power, the linear motors, the mass driver, the anchor lines, the suspended solar array, space elevators and the return to Earth, the cryogenic system, debris and repair, the casing budget, rings at other worlds, mission classes, and the open questions.

Detailed table of contents and sample excerpts will be posted as the draft is finalized.
