## About the book

*Orbital Ring Engineering* works through the physics, materials science and engineering needed to build an orbital ring. It states its assumptions and tests them step by step.

It invites readers to think about the challenges and to contribute. What are the optimal numbers? Are the assumptions correct?

## Inside the book

The first edition runs to eight chapters across three sections:

- **Section 1: Introduction and historical overview.** The evolution into space and the case for an orbital ring.
- **Section 2: Technical design.** Escaping the gravity well, momentum transfer and levitation, returning to Earth, anchor lines, and accelerating and deploying the ring.
- **Section 3: Applications and context.** Utilization, economics, and the political reasoning behind building one.

## A note on this edition

This is the original single-volume edition. The work has since been expanded into a three-volume edition, *Orbital Ring Engineering*, with significantly more detail and updated analysis. If you are buying for the first time, you may prefer the new edition.
