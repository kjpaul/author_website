## Privacy

This site belongs to Paul G. de Jong, the author of the books on it. It collects only what you type into its forms. It has no advertising, no tracking cookies and no accounts.

Last updated 23 November 2026.

## What the site collects

- **The release news form.** Your email address.
- **The advance copy application.** Your name, your email address and the answers you give on the form.
- **The errata form.** The error you report. Your name and your email address are optional.

## What it is used for

- **Release news.** One email when each book goes on sale, and an occasional note on how the books are going.
- **Advance copies.** One email to confirm your address. If you are sent a copy: the download link and three short notes about the book. You are added to the release news list only if you tick that box on the form.
- **Errata.** To check the error and fix it. If you give an email address, I may write to you with a question about your report. The address is never published. Confirmed errors are listed on the errata page. If you give a name, I may thank you by that name in a list of corrections on this site.

I do not sell your details, and I do not give them to anyone except the services named below, which need them to do their part.

## Where it is kept and who else handles it

- **MailerLite** sends my email and holds the release news list and the advance copy applicants.
- **Google Firebase** hosts this site and holds its database: the advance copy applications and the errata reports. A copy of each application and each report is emailed to me.
- **Cloudflare** carries the site's traffic, counts visits, and runs the verification box on the advance copy and errata forms, which keeps automated submissions out. The visit count uses no cookies and does not identify you.
- **BookFunnel** delivers the advance copies. I send you a download link. I do not pass your name or address to BookFunnel. If you use its app or ask it for help, its own privacy policy applies.
- **Google Fonts** supplies the typefaces. Your browser fetches them from Google when a page loads.

## Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set a cookie that it needs for security.

## Unsubscribing and deleting your details

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. To see what I hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, write to me through the [contact page](/contact/) and I will do it.
